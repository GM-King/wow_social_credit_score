Installation

At any level, you can read and write reviews of other players in your faction. Reviews received while leveling count toward the player's score.

Setup

Install the addon

  1. Download and extract SocialCreditSystem-2.11.0.zip.
  2. Move the extracted SocialCreditSystem folder into _forever_/Interface/AddOns/.
  3. Enable Social Credit System in the AddOns list at character selection, then enter the game.

Find someone you've played with

Click the minimap stamp. Recent lists your last 100 distinct party, raid, and whisper contacts. Reviewed lists players you currently have a review for. Click a name in either tab to open the profile.

Read and write reviews

Open another player's profile to read individual reviews as they arrive. To leave your own, choose a rating in half-star steps, add a short note, and save. Return through Reviewed to edit or delete your review, including during combat or in an instance.

Understand the score

A player's score appears once they reach level 60 and have reviews from five distinct authors. Reviews received before 60 count automatically. On the 0–1000 scale, ratings contribute 75% and dungeon and raid performance contributes 25%. Group Finder shows available scores for players and group leaders. An asterisk marks a provisional score while combat data is limited.

New in 2.11.0

Open Guilds from the minimap menu to see known same-faction guilds on your server ranked by their average member score. Each guild needs at least five scored members to appear.

Type /scs self to view your personal stats and, at level 60, your current dungeon or raid. Hover over an instance bracket to see lifetime DPS and HPS.

SCS now discovers peers through guilds, parties, raids, and direct profile lookups. Reviews sync by addon whisper.

Black Mark

Use 0 stars · Black Mark to flag a serious concern for your guild. Once confirmed, the review marks that player’s profile and portrait and flags them in Blizzard or Prat chat and Group Finder. You and your current guildmates see the mark. To read the review from chat, right-click the player’s name and choose Open SCS.
