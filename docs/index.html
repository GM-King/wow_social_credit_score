Installation

Browse and review at any level. Scores unlock at level 60.

Setup

Install the addon

  1. Download and extract SocialCreditSystem-2.9.0.zip.
  2. Move the extracted SocialCreditSystem folder into _forever_/Interface/AddOns/.
  3. Enable Social Credit System in the AddOns list. In game, click the minimap stamp, type /scs with a player targeted, or right-click a name in chat or the Who list and choose Open SCS.

Read and write reviews

Rate an eligible player in half-star steps and add a short note. The original author can edit or delete their review. Other eligible players can read synced reviews. The reviewed player cannot open their own profile.

Understand the score

At level 60, five reviews unlock the star average and a score out of 1000. Reviews received before level 60 count automatically. Reviews contribute 75%. Instance performance contributes 25% and uses the stronger DPS or HPS benchmark for the player’s class, talent tree, tank or non-tank role, and group size. Scores stay provisional while combat data is limited.

New in 2.9.0

The minimap stamp opens Recent and Reviewed. Recent shows up to 100 party, raid, and whisper contacts. Open verified profiles directly from the Who list, and browse profiles and save reviews during combat and in instances.

Blacklist

Use 0 stars · Blacklist to flag a serious concern for your guild. Once confirmed, the review marks that player’s profile and portrait and flags them in Blizzard or Prat chat and Group Finder. You and your current guildmates see the mark. To read the review from chat, right-click the player’s name and choose Open SCS.
